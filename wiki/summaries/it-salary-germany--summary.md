# German IT Salary Report 2026 — Summary

**Author**: Marvin Strathmann (heise online)
**Source**: heise.de
**Date**: April 2026
**Data Source**: Robert Half salary data (2025 data, 1,500 respondents)

## One-Paragraph Summary

German IT salaries in 2026 are in a "reweighting" phase: AI has created new job profiles (KI-Engineer, Automation Specialist, etc.) but these don't yet command a premium over comparable traditional roles. Geopolitical factors (Iran conflict, energy prices, economic uncertainty) are suppressing salary growth. The highest-paid roles remain C-suite (CIO: 125K€ median; up to 200K€ at 75th percentile) and architects (Software Architect: 94.7K€; Network/Cloud Architect: ~92K€).

## Key Insights

- AI roles (KI-Engineer, Automation Specialist) have **no significant salary premium** over general developers yet
- Senior AI Full-Stack Developer: 50K€ (25th percentile) to 95K€ (75th percentile) — high variance suggests experience/seniority matters greatly
- Architects (software, network, cloud) consistently earn ~20-30% more than their technical contributors
- Consulting/ERP/SAP: median ~85K€; many reach 100K€+ at 75th percentile
- Geopolitical factors (Iran conflict, energy costs) creating defensive posture on salary increases
