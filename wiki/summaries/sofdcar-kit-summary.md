# Summary: Software-Defined Car Foundations (SofDCar Project)

**Source**: Karlsruhe Institute of Technology press release
**Author**: KIT (Iris Oberdorf, STS)

**Published**: 2021 | **Clipped**: 2026-05-02
**Type**: Research project announcement

---

## Key Takeaways

**The problem**: Vehicles have 100+ control units. Without standardized update management and security methodology, individual programs interfere, safe OTA becomes impossible, and the accelerated development of new functions is blocked.

**SofDCar project partners**: Bosch (consortium leader), ETAS, Mercedes-Benz, T-Systems, Vector Informatik, ZF Friedrichshafen; research: KIT, University of Stuttgart, FKFS, FZI. Funded by Germany's Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs.

**Key research areas**:
- **IT reference architecture** for future vehicles (KIT + Stuttgart)
- **Extended digital twin**: Unified data flow from manufacturing through end-of-life; encompasses cloud domain, apps, backend systems, development systems. Goal: single unbroken information flow of vehicle data and software versions.
- **Security and dependability** (KIT KASTEL): Secure OTA updates; customer-specific vehicle configurations; AI-based security checks of components; identity and access administration
- **5G test track** (Campus Vaihingen, Stuttgart): Real-time testing of vehicles and setups in near-real conditions
- **Customer experience research** (FKFS driving simulator): Impact of online software updates on driving experience; early detection and prevention of component failures in EVs

**Core ambition**: Standardized rules and processes for controlling all software updates/upgrades, plus a consistent functional and IT security methodology. Prerequisites for accelerated, safe function delivery.
