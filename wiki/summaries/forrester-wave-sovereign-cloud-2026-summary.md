## Key Takeaways

### Sovereignty ≠ Data Residency — Beware "Sovereignty-Washing"
The most important conceptual point in the report: technical data residency is necessary but not sufficient. True cloud sovereignty requires three independent layers:
1. **Technical** — data stored in defined geography, encryption key control
2. **Operational** — support, patching, and monitoring by legally independent personnel
3. **Legal** — corporate structure insulated from foreign court orders (e.g., US CLOUD Act)

Global vendors achieve legal independence via local joint ventures or national partner clouds (Microsoft + Bleu/Delos, Google/S3NS + Thales). Without all three layers, vendors are "sovereignty-washing."

### US Hyperscalers Still Lead — But the Gap Is Closing
Google Cloud, Microsoft, and AWS are all Wave Leaders — the same companies whose dominance is cited as the European dependency problem. They're leading the sovereign cloud market *and* the regular cloud market simultaneously. This is the paradox: the cure (sovereign cloud from US vendors) still involves dependency on US companies, just with more legal/structural insulation.

### Oracle's Unique Pricing Model
Oracle is the only Leader that prices sovereign options identically to non-sovereign equivalents — no premium. Every other vendor charges extra for sovereignty controls. This is a genuine differentiator for budget-sensitive regulated industries.

### The EU-Native Alternatives Are Real But Limited
Several EU-native players (T-Systems, STACKIT, OVHcloud, Aruba, S3NS) exist and are credible for specific use cases — but none offers hyperscaler-grade service breadth. Trade-off is explicit: more sovereignty = fewer available services. For most enterprise workloads, EU-native clouds require accepting a reduced feature set.

**Notable EU standouts**:
- **S3NS** (Thales + Google JV): Received France's SecNumCloud certification December 2025 — the strictest EU sovereignty certification. Good for French-regulated organizations wanting hyperscale capabilities.
- **STACKIT** (Schwarz Group/Lidl): Data exclusively Germany/Austria; open-source foundations; ISO 27001/BSI C5/GDPR; transparent pay-as-you-go pricing. Emerging credible alternative.
- **T-Systems**: Building open-source VMware alternative (2026) — significant if delivered.

### Huawei Cloud: The Mirror Problem
Huawei Cloud is a Contender — strong Chinese public cloud capabilities, but its sovereign options are "the Achilles' heel." Huawei AI chips are banned in the US and being phased out in the EU. European organizations won't touch it for sovereignty needs. This is the geopolitical mirror image of European organizations' concerns about US hyperscalers — China faces the same European scrutiny US vendors do.

### Air-Gapped AI Is a Differentiator
Google Cloud's air-gapped environment is the only one with advanced AI services (Gemini, Vertex AI) in a fully disconnected deployment. Most air-gapped offerings are compute/storage only. The ability to run frontier AI in a fully isolated environment is a genuine capability gap that competitors haven't closed.
