# Running Season Planning (Laufsaison Planung) — Summary

**Author**: Unknown (running-culture.de)
**Source**: running-culture.de
**Date**: January (year not specified)
**Language**: German

## One-Paragraph Summary

A framework for planning a full running season with a September/autumn marathon as the primary goal. The season divides into four phases: winter base (cross-training + 1-2 runs/week), spring form check (5K/10K + HM race), marathon block (10-14 weeks of specific training), and race week. The framework emphasizes focus (max 2 annual goals), periodization, and building a 1-2 week buffer for illness or minor injury.

## Seasonal Structure

| Phase | Timing | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Winter Base | Dec–Feb | Cross-training (ski, cycling), strength/yoga 3-4x/week, 1-2 runs/week |
| Spring Form Check | Mar–Apr | 5K or 10K time trial; then 8-12 week prep HM race |
| Marathon Block | May–Aug | 10-14 weeks specific training; 3-4 runs/week; test races mid-block |
| Race Week | Final week | Regeneration; calm; no new training stimuli |

## Key Principles

- Plan for max 2 key events per year — don't dilute focus
- Build 1-2 week buffer into marathon block (illness/injury insurance)
- Mid-block test races: 10K + HM at near-marathon goal pace to validate fitness
- 1-2 weeks recovery after any race before resuming training
- Winter cross-training maintains cardiovascular base without running injury risk
