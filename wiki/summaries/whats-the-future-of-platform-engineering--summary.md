# What's the Future of Platform Engineering? — Summary

**Author**: Heather Joslyn (The New Stack)
**Source**: thenewstack.io
**Date**: March 2025

## One-Paragraph Summary

Platform engineering faces adoption challenges because organizations focus on how to build an IDP rather than why they need one. "10% technical, 90% cultural change" — and the cultural side consistently trips up organizations. AI/agentic AI is creating new demands (GPU orchestration, model serving) while also representing a new abstraction layer in the platform stack. Gartner predicts 80% of software engineering orgs will have a platform team by 2026, but most are fledgling.

## Key Claims

- "Not all platforms are created equal; not all platform engineering teams are created equal"
- Platform engineering is suffering from the same pattern as DevOps: "Is this a thing I buy? Is this a team I hire?"
- 10% technical, 90% cultural change — and cultural change takes time, hard questions, introspection
- AI agents may become users of the platform (GPU orchestration) or another abstraction layer within it
- Humanitec experimenting with AI-integrated platform orchestrator: query the platform, it performs tasks using available tools
- Technologies and tools reinforce and amplify existing culture — "the best way to change culture is to change how people work"
