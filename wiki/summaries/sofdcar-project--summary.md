# Summary: Software-Defined Car (SofDCar) Project

**Source**: KIT (Karlsruhe Institute of Technology), 2021
**URL**: https://www.kit.edu/kit/english/pi_2021_099_software-defined-car-foundations-for-future-vehicle-generations.php

---

## One-Paragraph Summary

The SofDCar project is a German government-funded (BMWi) research consortium led by Bosch with partners including Mercedes-Benz, ZF, T-Systems, KIT, and University of Stuttgart. Its goal is to develop standardized rules and processes for controlling all software updates/upgrades in future vehicles, along with consistent functional and IT security methodology. The project introduces an extended digital twin covering the entire vehicle lifecycle (manufacture to end-of-life) across vehicle, cloud, apps, and backend systems — far beyond traditional design-phase-only digital twins.

---

## Key Insights

- **Extended digital twin**: Covers development + runtime data, vehicle + cloud + apps + backend, from manufacture to scrappage — first time this full scope has been attempted
- **Security as prerequisite**: Software functions must be updatable easily, securely, and dependably post-purchase; various customer-specific configurations must be considered
- **5G test track**: University of Stuttgart building real-time 5G campus network for close-to-reality testing of vehicles and SDV setups
- **Edge backend focus**: Research on IT reference architecture for information nodes outside vehicles in future IT infrastructure
- **AI security**: FZI researching AI-based methods for securing vehicle components + practical security checks
- **Driving simulator studies**: Testing impact of online software updates on driving experience (comfort, safety of autonomous features)
- **Innovation Campus "Future Mobility"**: KIT + University of Stuttgart platform interconnecting research, implementation, and qualification
