# Summary: Build the Platform Engineering Team

**Source**: Microsoft Learn — "Build the Platform Engineering Team"
**Author**: juliakm (Microsoft Platform Engineering)
**URL**: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/platform-engineering/team
**Published**: (n.d.) | **Clipped**: 2026-06-15
**Type**: Organizational guidance

---

## Key Takeaways

**Reactive vs. proactive culture**: Reactive = lone developers build tools in silos; knowledge leaves when the developer leaves. Proactive = tools built and maintained by a team; managed autonomy for developers; new projects start quickly. Platform engineering requires executive buy-in and a proactive culture.

**Maturity model (4 levels)**:
- **Provisional**: Data-driven decisions; recognize inefficiencies; enhance org structure
- **Operational**: Collaborative mindset; robust feedback mechanisms; culture of reliability
- **Scalable**: Product-led growth; culture of empathy; user research in decision-making
- **Optimizing**: Innovation and autonomy; teams address edge cases; continual relevance

**Organization structure**: Platform engineers are the "glue between development and operations." Team Topologies model recommended. Need: sponsors (executives), stakeholders (ops/security/compliance/architecture), a product manager role.

**The talent gap**: "It's very difficult to hire really good infrastructure and platform engineers." Most candidates are passionate about customer-facing applications, not infrastructure. Finding people passionate about infra is a rare skill.

**Skill profile**: Platform engineers need product mindset + operations understanding. Backgrounds: developers, IT operations, Kubernetes admins, SREs, IaC experts. Best hires from existing application teams who can "represent the voice of the customer."
