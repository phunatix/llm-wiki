# Summary: Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg

**Source**: Wikipedia — "Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg"
**Clipped**: 2026-04-16

---

## What This Source Contains

A comprehensive Wikipedia article covering the planning, site selection, construction, production ramp, labor relations, incidents, and opposition surrounding Tesla's first European manufacturing plant in Grünheide, Brandenburg, Germany.

---

## Key Takeaways

### Competitive Site Selection Mirrors Strategic Dependency
Over **10 European countries** competed aggressively to win the Gigafactory. The underlying driver: Asia controlled **88% of global battery manufacturing capacity** as of 2018. European governments viewed hosting a Gigafactory as strategic industrial infrastructure — the automotive equivalent of the digital AI dependency pattern covered in [[summaries/us-cuts-off-tech-to-europe--summary]]. Germany won with renewable energy credentials, workforce, and government incentives.

### Production Ramp Has Been Slow
Giga Berlin was planned for 500,000 vehicles/year but opened March 2022 and as of August 2025 still had not consistently hit 250,000 (half capacity). Targets were actually *lowered* in mid-2023. This illustrates the gap between Tesla's announced production ambitions and actual execution.

### Significant Labor and Safety Concerns
IG Metall organized at the plant; workers reported extreme workload intensity, sickness rates up to 30%, and high work accident rates — roughly 3x the rate at a comparable Audi factory. Tesla threatened employees with termination for union sticker campaigns. This is at odds with Tesla's public positioning.

### Persistent Protest and Sabotage
Two separate arson attacks by the left-wing extremist group Vulkangruppe (2021 and 2024). Environmental activists built treehouses adjacent to the site; stormed the factory during a 2024 protest. Tesla preemptively closed the factory. This level of sustained opposition is unusual for a single manufacturing facility.

### CrowdStrike Incident
The July 2024 CrowdStrike-related IT outage affected production at Giga Berlin — the factory was briefly halted. Tesla removed CrowdStrike software from plant computers as a result.
