# What If the US Cuts Off Tech to Europe? — Summary

**Author**: Msukhareva (Substack / AI Realist)
**Source**: msukhareva.substack.com
**Date**: January 2026

## One-Paragraph Summary

A critical analysis of Europe's structural dependency on US cloud and AI infrastructure, framed around the hypothetical scenario of US-EU tech decoupling. 70% of European cloud capacity and 85% of AI GPUs are US-controlled. Europe has produced no competitive AI chips, one modestly competitive AI model (Mistral), and instead focused on regulation (EU AI Act) rather than infrastructure. China's open-weight model strategy (DeepSeek, Kimi, MiniMax) provides an alternative path, but Europe has not built the energy, talent, or startup ecosystem to compete with either.

## Key Data

- **70%** of EU cloud capacity: US providers
- **85%** of EU AI GPUs: US providers
- European electricity: up to 3x US cost — structural AI disadvantage
- Mistral: only notable European AI model

## Key Insight

> "If you can't innovate, you regulate."

The EU AI Act regulates AI that doesn't exist in Europe, while ignoring structural problems: energy cost, talent flight to US salaries, bureaucracy strangling startups.

## China as the Alternative

Chinese open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi K2, MiniMax M2) are strategically positioned as the anti-closed-AI alternative. Europe has de facto more AI sovereignty via Chinese open models than via regulation of US models.
