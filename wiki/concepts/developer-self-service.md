# Summary

Developer self-service is the ability for developers to perform routine delivery and infrastructure tasks without waiting on centralized operators. The current sources treat it as valuable but easy to misuse, especially when teams confuse empowerment with dumping operational burden onto developers or when abstractions become controlling cages instead of helpful paths.

# Key Ideas

- Self-service should reduce waiting and cognitive overhead, not merely reassign toil.
- Golden paths work best when developers understand what sits underneath them and retain some freedom to go lower-level when necessary.
- Cultural communication and feedback loops are essential because the right level of abstraction varies by team.
