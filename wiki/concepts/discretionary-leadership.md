# Discretionary Leadership

**Domain**: Leadership / Organizational Scale

**One-line definition**: Apple's evolved leadership model for managing at scale — leaders deliberately choose where to own deeply, teach others, learn new areas, or delegate, rather than trying to be expert in everything.

## Definition

As Apple scaled (8K → 137K employees), leaders could no longer be immersed in all details. Discretionary leadership is the framework Apple developed to preserve the experts-leading-experts model while managing much larger and more diverse organizations.

## The Four Modes

| Mode | When to Use | How Leader Behaves |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Own** | Deep expertise, highest-value activities | Fully engaged, makes decisions, drives work |
| **Teach** | Deep expertise, but delegating execution | Strong critiques, mentoring, setting standards |
| **Learn** | New area outside original expertise | Beginner's mindset; asking questions to understand, not to evaluate |
| **Delegate** | Not an expert; others must lead | Sets objectives, monitors progress, holds accountable — "general management" |

## Example: Roger Rosner (VP Applications)

Time allocation:
- **40% Own**: Core software application architecture and UX
- **30% Learn**: News app (publishing, digital advertising, machine learning personalization)
- **15% Teach**: Traditional productivity apps (Keynote, Pages) — trains others to carry these forward
- **15% Delegate**: GarageBand, iMovie — not his core expertise

## Key Insight

Most Apple VPs spend most time in **own** and **learn** boxes. Most general managers at other companies spend most time in the **delegate** box.

The learn mode is particularly important: it requires a genuine beginner's mindset, questioning subordinates as if you don't already know the answer — because you don't. This differs starkly from own or teach behavior.

## Why This Preserves Functional Organization at Scale

- Experts can expand scope by entering "learning mode" for new areas
- Teams grow without leaders needing to be experts in everything
- The "delegate" mode (classical general management) is a last resort, not the default
