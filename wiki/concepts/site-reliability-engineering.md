# Summary

Site Reliability Engineering is an engineering discipline centered on reliability, automation, efficiency, and learning from failure. The current sources present it both as a production engineering role and as a transferable mindset that can be generalized beyond software systems.

# Key Ideas

- Reliability is a product property that must be balanced against velocity, cost, and effort rather than maximized blindly.
- SRE work emphasizes toil reduction, systemic fixes, observability, and blameless learning.
- The same mental model can be abstracted into broader frameworks for operating complex systems outside traditional software infrastructure.
