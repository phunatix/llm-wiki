# Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE)

**Domain**: Engineering Roles / Platform Engineering

**One-line definition**: An engineer embedded with customers or internal user teams to help adopt, configure, and operationalize complex software — a hybrid of software engineer, technical consultant, and customer success manager.

## Definition

The term was coined by Palantir, borrowing from military terminology: a forward-deployed soldier is stationed where action is happening, ready for rapid response. In tech, the FDE sits between back-office engineering and customer-facing roles. They're deployed to client sites (or internal teams) to help with adoption, configuration, data preparation, and real-world complexity.

## Role Description

- Remains hands-on throughout the customer lifecycle (not just design and handoff)
- Bridges platform/product and users; translates technical capability into user outcomes
- Works toward clear "missions" tied to specific business outcomes
- Involved in daily operations, real-time problem solving, and system adjustment

## Key Skills

1. **Full-stack technical depth**: Can dive into code, architecture, and systems
2. **Listening and user empathy**: Tight feedback loops; good at hearing what users actually need
3. **Communication and writing**: Specifications, user stories, documentation — "garbage in, garbage out" applies to AI tools too
4. **Business outcome orientation**: Success measured by adoption, retention, and business metrics
5. **Prototyping speed**: PoC → user feedback → iteration; "people don't know what they want until they see something they don't want"
6. **Pattern library**: Skills and experiences that transfer across many contexts (consultant mindset)

## FDE for AI (AI FDE)

The AI FDE lives in "ambiguity by default":
- Fine-tuning of models for specific organizational data
- LLM deployment and RAG systems
- Data integration across enterprise silos
- 95% of AI pilots fail due to data silo and integration problems (MIT NANDA, 2025)
- Data — not models — defines the next wave of competitive edge (Thorsten Walther, MongoDB)

## FDE for Internal Platform Engineering

Embedding platform engineers within application teams:
- Get candid feedback from colleagues (internal users)
- Test new patterns before wider rollout
- Jumpstart major migrations in safe beta teams
- Builds trust that the platform is built from lived experience

## Career Note

The FDE role may evolve or disappear (like prompt engineering) as AI tooling matures and data silos break down. The durable skill is connecting engineering work to user needs and business outcomes — not the FDE role itself.
