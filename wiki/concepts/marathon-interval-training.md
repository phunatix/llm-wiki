# Marathon Interval Training

**Domain**: Running / Marathon Training

**One-line definition**: Structured tempo intervals planned backwards from race day, progressing from 10K pace → half-marathon pace → marathon pace across the training block.

## The Laufcampus Six Race Tempos

| Abbreviation | Distance           | Interval Lengths | Max Volume    |
| ------------ | ------------------ | ---------------- | ------------- |
| 1RT          | 1K race pace       | Short bursts     | -             |
| 3RT          | 3K race pace       | Short            | -             |
| 5RT          | 5K race pace       | Medium           | -             |
| 10RT         | 10K race pace      | 1,000–2,000m     | 10,000m total |
| HMRT         | Half-marathon pace | 1,600–3,000m     | 14,000m total |
| MRT          | Marathon pace      | 3,000–6,000m     | 24,000m total |

## The Backwards Planning Principle

Train race tempo **last** in the preparation block. Work backwards:
- Weeks 11-15 (from race): Train at 10RT (above race pace, builds speed)
- Weeks 6-10: Train at HMRT (threshold, builds lactate clearance)
- Weeks 1-5: Train at MRT (race specificity, builds confidence)
- Race week: Short HMRT session → marathon

## Sample Final Phase Schedule (15 weeks to race)

| Week | Session |
|------|---------|
| Race week | 3 × 2,000m @ HMRT |
| Week 15 | 3 × 6,000m @ MRT |
| Week 14 | 4 × 4,000m @ MRT |
| Week 13 | 3 × 5,000m @ MRT |
| Week 12 | 3 × 4,000m @ MRT |
| Week 11 | 4 × 3,000m @ MRT |

## Three Key Weekly Sessions

1. **Long Run (Langer Lauf)**: Base endurance, fat adaptation
2. **Threshold Run (Zügiger Dauerlauf)**: Sustained effort, aerobic threshold
3. **Interval Training**: Race-specific speed, as described above

## Key Insight: Race Week HMRT Session

Running 2km intervals at HMRT pace on Tue/Wed of race week:
- "Primes" neuromuscular system with speed feeling
- Makes marathon pace feel easy by comparison at race start
- Helps avoid starting too fast (the #1 marathon mistake)
