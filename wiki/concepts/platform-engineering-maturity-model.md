# Summary

The platform engineering maturity model is a framework for assessing how an organization's platform discipline is evolving across several dimensions. It is intended to surface the next improvements worth pursuing rather than assign a simplistic overall grade.

# Key Ideas

- Maturity should be evaluated independently across multiple aspects rather than as one global level.
- The model's aspects are investment, adoption, interfaces, operations, and measurement.
- The stages progress from provisional to operational to scalable to optimizing.
- Greater maturity usually demands more resources and coordination, so advancement should be justified by context and outcomes.
