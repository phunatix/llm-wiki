# Tesla, Inc.

## Overview

Tesla, Inc. is an American multinational automotive and clean energy company headquartered in **Austin, Texas**. It designs, manufactures, and sells battery electric vehicles (BEVs), stationary battery energy storage, solar panels and solar shingles, and related services.

- **Founded**: July 1, 2003 (incorporated as Tesla Motors) by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning
- **CEO since 2008**: Elon Musk (joined as chairman 2004, became CEO 2008)
- **Headquarters**: Austin, Texas (moved from Palo Alto 2021)
- **IPO**: June 29, 2010 — NASDAQ; first US automaker IPO since Ford (1956)
- **Most valuable automaker in the world**: since July 2020 (by market cap)
- **$1 trillion market cap**: first achieved October 2021; again November 2024–February 2025; again May 2025+

## Key Corporate Milestones

- **2004**: Elon Musk leads Series A ($7.5M); joins as chairman
- **2009**: Government loan ($465M) enables Model S; repaid May 2013 with $12M interest
- **2010**: Acquires former NUMMI plant in Fremont, CA from Toyota ($42M)
- **2013**: NASDAQ-100 inclusion
- **2014**: Autopilot driver-assistance announced
- **2015**: Powerwall and Powerpack energy storage unveiled
- **2016**: Acquires SolarCity ($2.6B all-stock); creates Tesla Energy subsidiary. Unveils Model 3 prototype (325K+ reservations in one week)
- **2017**: Renames to "Tesla, Inc." (from Tesla Motors)
- **2018**: Model 3 "production hell"; Musk's "taking private" tweet → SEC fraud charge, $20M fine, chairman resignation
- **2020**: Added to S&P 500 (most valuable addition ever); 740% share price gain
- **2021**: Bitcoin investment ($1.5B); briefly accepted BTC payments (49 days); sold 75% of holdings 2022
- **2021**: Moved legal HQ to Texas
- **2023**: Cybertruck deliveries begin; NACS charging standard adopted across major US EV makers
- **2024**: 10% employee layoff; incorporation moved from Delaware to Texas; Cybercab and Robovan unveiled; Delaware court rejects Musk's $56B pay package
- **2025**: Stock falls 7 consecutive weeks (Feb–Mar) tied to Musk's political activities; multiple protests and vandalism globally; Grok AI integrated into vehicles (July); Robotaxi service launched in Austin
- **Jan 2026**: Lost title of world's largest BEV manufacturer to Chinese BYD (BYD: 2.26M vs Tesla: 1.64M BEV sales for full year 2025)
- **Jan 2026**: Musk announces Model S and Model X discontinuation in Q2 2026 to focus on Optimus robot manufacturing
- **Mar 2026**: Unveiled Tesla/xAI joint project pairing Grok LLM with Tesla AI agent for screen/keyboard control

## Energy Business (Tesla Energy)

Tesla Energy (subsidiary, created from SolarCity merger 2016) sells:
- **Powerwall**: Home battery storage
- **Megapack**: Grid-scale battery storage
- **Solar panels / Solar Roof**: Solar shingles and traditional panels
- **Tesla Electric**: Virtual power plant in Texas (manages Powerwalls, sells back to grid)

In 2023: Deployed **14.7 GWh** of battery storage (+125% YoY) but only 223 MW of solar (-36% YoY).

## Charging Network (Supercharger)

- Launched September 2012 alongside Model S
- As of November 2025: **~7,900 stations**, **75,000+ connectors** globally
- Regional distribution: Asia-Pacific 3,000 stations, North America 3,000, Europe 1,500
- Current max output: **500 kW**
- NACS (North American Charging Standard): Adopted by virtually all major US/Canadian EV makers 2023–2024 — a strategic recurring revenue source for Tesla

## Technology Highlights

### Battery Strategy
Tesla uses four cell formats: 18650 (Model S/X), 2170 (Model 3/Y), 4680 (Model Y/Cybertruck at Giga Texas), and prismatic (LFP for some RWD variants). Suppliers: CATL, LG Energy Solution, Panasonic. Battery cost estimated at $112/kWh in 2021 vs. $132/kWh industry average.

### Vertical Integration
Unlike most automakers who outsource ~80% of components, Tesla develops batteries, motors, and software in-house. Described as "vertically integrated" — unusual in automotive.

### Autopilot / FSD
- Introduced 2014; hardware shipping to all cars since September 2014
- Basic Autopilot **discontinued January 22, 2026** (US/Canada); replaced with Traffic Aware Cruise Control only
- Full Self-Driving (FSD): Available as monthly subscription only as of February 14, 2026
- Camera-only (Vision) since May 2021 in Model Y/3 — no radar

### Robotics
- **Optimus robot**: A key focus as of 2026; Model S/X discontinued to redirect manufacturing capacity to Optimus
- **Terafab** (March 2026): Joint project with SpaceX and xAI to build a mega-semiconductor fab targeting 1 terawatt of AI compute capacity/year

## Business Model Distinctives

- **Direct-to-consumer sales**: No franchise dealerships; sells via website and company-owned stores (~400 stores in 35+ countries); first US automaker to do this since widespread franchise adoption
- **No PR department**
- **Continuous hardware updates**: Ships updates outside model-year cadence (unlike traditional automakers)
- **Open patents**: Licenses technology to competitors to accelerate EV adoption globally
