# Kaspar von Grünberg

**Category**: Person (Tech Founder / Platform Engineering Pioneer)

**Quick Summary**: CEO and founder of Humanitec; credited with coining the term "Internal Developer Platform" (IDP); leading voice in the platform engineering community.

## Overview

Kaspar von Grünberg founded Humanitec and coined the term "Internal Developer Platform" (IDP). He has spent over a decade building IDPs at scale and is the author of several defining articles on platform engineering. He is a regular speaker at platform engineering events.

## Key Contributions

- Coined the term "Internal Developer Platform (IDP)"
- Founded [[entities/Humanitec]] and the Score specification
- Developed the cognitive load model for evaluating self-service platforms
- Documented the two failure modes of developer self-service ("throw everything at them" vs. "take it all from them")
