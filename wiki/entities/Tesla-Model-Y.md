# Tesla Model Y

## Overview

The Tesla Model Y is a compact crossover SUV and Tesla's best-selling vehicle. It became the **world's best-selling car overall** in 2023 — the first EV to claim that title — with 1.21 million units sold that year. Production launched in March 2020 at Fremont, California, and the platform shares approximately 76% of components with the [[entities/Obsidian|Model 3]] (sibling sedan).

Cumulative global sales 2021–2024: ~2.16 million units. The Model Y is produced at four Gigafactories: Fremont (CA), Shanghai, Berlin-Brandenburg, and Austin (Texas).

## Key Technical Details

- **Drag coefficient**: 0.23 (standard) / 0.216 (Model Y L)
- **Heat pump system**: Uses "Octovalve" / "Super Manifold" — a single thermal management unit covering cabin, battery, and motors. Improves cold-weather efficiency significantly.
- **Sensing**: Radar removed May 2021; now runs on **Tesla Vision** (camera-only) for Autopilot/FSD.
- **Manufacturing**: Giga Press die-casting produces single-piece rear underbody (replaces ~70 stamped parts), reducing cost and improving structural rigidity.
- **Battery cell types**: 2170 LNMC (standard/performance), Prismatic LFP (Standard RWD), 4680 LFP (some AWD variants from Giga Texas).
- **Cargo**: 76.2 cu ft / 2,158 L (standard); 89.7 cu ft / 2,539 L (Model Y L)

## Safety Concerns

- **Electrically-operated doors**: Interior door releases require a specific procedure; occupants may become trapped if unaware, especially in rear seats where some vehicles lack a manual release.
- **Hidden door handles**: Active safety debate. China has mandated Tesla change the hidden handle design by **January 1, 2027** or exit the market. Other jurisdictions are reviewing similar bans.
- **NHTSA investigation (Sept 2025)**: 174,000+ Model Y units (2021) investigated for door handles that may prevent exit after parking, in some cases trapping children inside.

## Markets

- **United States**: Primary production at Giga Texas; Canadian-market vehicles built in China from Apr 2023 to free up US-manufactured units for federal EV tax credit eligibility.
- **China**: Gigafactory Shanghai; first Chinese delivery Jan 18, 2021. Sold out Q1 2021 production in 6 days. In June 2024, became first Tesla eligible for Chinese government vehicle procurement.
- **Australia / NZ**: Deliveries from Aug 2022 (Shanghai). First export market for the Model Y L outside China (Mar 2026). Performance variant now built at Giga Berlin.
- **India**: Launched July 15, 2025, imported from China.
