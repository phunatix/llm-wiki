# Humanitec

**Category**: Company (Software / Platform Engineering)

**Quick Summary**: Pioneer in the Internal Developer Platform (IDP) space; coined the term "IDP"; makes the Platform Orchestrator product and the Score specification.

## Overview

Humanitec is a platform engineering company founded by [[entities/Kaspar-von-Grunberg]], who is credited with coining the term "Internal Developer Platform" (IDP). The company produces the Platform Orchestrator, a tool for building dynamic IDPs, and the Score specification for workload configuration. Humanitec is one of the most prominent voices in the platform engineering community and publishes influential research on DevOps and developer self-service practices.

## Key Products

- **Platform Orchestrator v2**: Simplified architecture, supports any compute target; replaces v1
- **Score**: Open specification for developer-centric workload configuration
- **platformengineering.org**: Community resource for the platform engineering discipline

## Key Research & Publications

- Annual "State of Platform Engineering" surveys
- Research on DevOps team setups (1,856 engineering teams analyzed)
  - 21.2%: Full "you build it, you run it"
  - 32.2%: Traditional throw-over-the-fence setup
  - 44.6%: Torn-down walls, but overwhelming developers with delivery tasks

## Key Insights from Humanitec Sources

- Self-service done wrong creates two failure modes: "throw everything at them" (cognitive overload) vs. "take it all from them" (golden cage / black box)
- 97% of developers follow a golden path once established and trusted
- Division of labor is inevitable at scale — the question is where the handoff point is, not whether ops should exist
- Communication is the single most important component of successful self-service
