# Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg

## Overview

Gigafactory Berlin-Brandenburg (also **Giga Berlin**, **Gigafactory 4**) is Tesla's first and only manufacturing plant in Europe, located in **Grünheide (Mark)**, Brandenburg, Germany — 35 km southeast of central Berlin. It is the manufacturing site for the [[entities/Tesla-Model-Y|Tesla Model Y]] (Performance variant) and handles battery, powertrain, and seat production in addition to final vehicle assembly.

- **Announced**: November 12, 2019 (Elon Musk at Das Goldene Lenkrad award show)
- **Ground broken**: February 2020
- **Official opening**: March 22, 2022
- **Production capacity**: 250,000 vehicles/year (current); 500,000 planned long-term
- **Employees**: ~12,000 (as of 2024)
- **Land area**: ~300 hectares (740 acres), purchased for ~€41 million

## Production Ramp

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Environmental approval granted | March 2022 |
| Official opening | March 22, 2022 |
| First Model Y Performance produced | April 29, 2022 |
| 1,000 units/week run rate | June 17, 2022 |
| 5,000 units/week run rate | March 25, 2023 |
| Reduced target (4,350/week) | July–Aug 2023 |

As of August 2025, Giga Berlin had still not consistently reached its target of 250,000 units/year — only half its full designed capacity of 500,000. Expansion plans remain on hold pending improved market conditions.

## Site & Logistics

- Located on the Berlin–Wrocław railway, providing rail freight access
- Shuttle train (Niederbarnimer Eisenbahn, 400-seat, diesel) runs between Fangschleuse station and factory since September 2023
- Surrounded by state-owned pine forest (monoculture); Tesla planted 265 ha of new multi-species forest as mitigation for 160 ha cleared
- Proximity to Berlin Brandenburg Airport (~20 km) cited as a site selection factor
- Water supply from Strausberg-Erkner; sufficient for first phase (250,000 vehicles), expansion requires additional infrastructure

---

## Site Selection Context

More than 10 European countries competed to host the Gigafactory (France, Netherlands, Czech Republic, Portugal, Spain, Finland, Lithuania, Estonia, Germany). Germany won with a combination of:
- Existing Tesla European HQ in the Netherlands (nearby)
- Qualified automotive workforce
- Brandenburg's high per-capita renewable energy production
- Expandable industrial-zoned greenfield land
- German government subsidies and investment incentives

A key strategic concern driving European countries' bids: Asia controlled **88% of global battery manufacturing capacity** in early 2018. European governments viewed Gigafactory investment as critical infrastructure to avoid battery dependency on Asia — the same logic as the broader [[concepts/europe-ai-dependency|Europe AI dependency]] pattern but applied to energy/automotive.

## Incidents & Controversies

- **2021**: Arson attack on transmitter tower by left-wing extremist group Vulkangruppe; group cited environmental concerns
- **September 2022**: Fire in recycling facility (800 m³ cardboard/wood); 50 firefighters, no injuries
- **March 2024**: Production halted for several days after suspected arson attack on nearby electricity substation, again claimed by Vulkangruppe
- **Early 2024**: "Stop Tesla" activists built treehouses adjacent to factory; planned 5-day protest in May 2024; dozens stormed factory grounds, clashed with police
- **July 2024**: CrowdStrike outage briefly halted factory operations; security software removed from plant computers

---

## Labor Relations

- Union IG Metall has organized campaigns at the plant; 1,000+ employees signed joint demands for improved conditions (2023)
- Workers report "extreme" workload due to short cycle times, insufficient staffing, excessive production targets
- Sickness/absenteeism rates reported as high as ~30%
- In December 2022, factory faced issues filling positions, retaining employees, and high absenteeism
- Tesla threatened termination without notice over union sticker campaign

---

## Opposition

- Local AfD (as of 2024) opposes expansion plans
- Environmental groups cite: water supply impacts, chemical waste disposal, wildlife displacement (reptiles, bats, birds in the pine forest)
- Several neighboring municipalities formed a coordination group to manage infrastructure and residential impacts (December 2019)

---

## Connection to Tesla Model Y

Giga Berlin primarily manufactures the [[entities/Tesla-Model-Y|Tesla Model Y]] Performance variant (AWD, 2170-cell). The Australian market's Model Y Performance was shifted to Giga Berlin production as of 2026. The Model Y RWD and Long Range variants sold in many European markets are also produced here.
