# Apple

**Category**: Organization (Technology Company)

**Quick Summary**: The defining example of a functional organizational structure maintained at massive scale; "experts lead experts" model enables continuous innovation without general managers.

## Overview

Apple is remarkable not just for its products but for its organizational design, which defies conventional management theory. Despite growing from ~8,000 employees in 1997 to 137,000+ in 2019, Apple has maintained a functional structure with no conventional general managers — the only intersection point for all functions on any product is the CEO.

## Key Organizational Facts

- **Structure**: Functional (not divisional/business-unit-based)
- **Single P&L**: Entire company runs on one P&L — no business unit P&Ls
- **No general managers**: SVPs lead functions (hardware, software, design), not products
- **CEO as only GM**: Only Tim Cook (and Steve Jobs before him) sits at the intersection of all functions
- **VPs**: ~50 in 2006 → ~96 in 2019 (doubled as company grew 8x)

## The Three Leadership Characteristics

1. **Deep expertise**: Leaders are experts in their domain, not professional managers. "Train an expert to manage, not a manager to be an expert."
2. **Immersion in details**: Leaders know their org three levels down. Detail familiarity enables fast cross-functional decisions.
3. **Collaborative debate**: No function owns a product — cross-functional debate resolves design decisions. "Accountability without control."

## The Discretionary Leadership Model

As Apple scaled, leaders evolved to manage across four modes:
- **Own**: Areas of deep expertise where leader is fully engaged
- **Teach**: Areas where leader mentors others to carry expertise forward
- **Learn**: New areas outside original expertise, requiring beginner mindset
- **Delegate**: Areas where others must lead; leader sets objectives and accountability

Roger Rosner (VP Applications) example: 40% own / 30% learn / 15% teach / 15% delegate.

## Historical Timeline

- **1997**: Steve Jobs returns; fires all BU general managers in one day; restructures to functional org
- **2007**: iPhone launch — product of deep cross-functional collaboration (40+ specialist teams for camera alone)
- **2016**: Portrait mode camera — canonical example of collaborative debate resolving competing design standards
- **2019**: 137,000 employees; $260B revenue; functional structure intact

## Key Insight

> "Structure follows strategy" (Chandler) — but Apple chose the opposite structure from what conventional theory predicts, and made it work by aligning decision rights with expertise rather than accountability.
