# Summary

This source synthesizes interviews with developers who already rely heavily on AI tools and argues that software development is moving from direct code production toward delegation, verification, and orchestration of agents. It presents a staged progression from AI skeptic to AI strategist and emphasizes enduring value in fundamentals, architecture, and review.

# Key Takeaways

- Developers using AI heavily tend to shift their effort toward setting context and validating results.
- The source frames AI fluency as an evolving skill that includes tool choice, prompting, planning, and review.
- It argues that education and assessment should adapt to AI-assisted workflows rather than ignore them.
