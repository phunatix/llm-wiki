# Summary

This source generalizes SRE principles into a reusable framework for designing reliable systems in many domains, from training programs to customer service and family emergency planning. It maps the service reliability hierarchy into domain-agnostic questions around observability, incident handling, learning, piloting, and scaling.

# Key Takeaways

- SRE is presented as a mindset about reliability and system design, not just a role tied to distributed systems.
- The framework separates aspiration from tactics and encourages explicit treatment of both the “what” and the “how.”
- Reliability is framed as a balancing act rather than a pursuit of perfection.
