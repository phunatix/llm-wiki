# Summary

This source describes Apple’s unusual large-scale functional organization and argues that innovation benefits when decision rights align with expertise rather than with divisional P&L ownership. It emphasizes the model of experts leading experts and contrasts it with conventional multidivisional management structures.

# Key Takeaways

- Apple retained a functional structure at scale rather than moving fully to business units.
- The model depends on deep expertise at leadership levels and intense cross-functional collaboration.
- The article distinguishes owning, learning, teaching, and delegating as different leadership modes in expert-led organizations.
