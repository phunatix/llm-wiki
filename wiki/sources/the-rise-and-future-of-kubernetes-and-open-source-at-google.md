# Summary

This source presents Google’s perspective on why Kubernetes became central to cloud-native computing, why open source mattered to that outcome, and why the next frontier includes software supply chain security, managed services, edge deployments, and hybrid cloud.

# Key Takeaways

- Kubernetes’ open-source path is presented as essential to its industry-wide adoption.
- Supply chain security and dependency governance are treated as foundational concerns for modern software.
- The source expects Kubernetes to keep expanding across edge, coprocessor-heavy, and hybrid environments.
