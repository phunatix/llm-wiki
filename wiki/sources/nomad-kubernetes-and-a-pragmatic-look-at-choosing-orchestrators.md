# Summary

This source argues against one-size-fits-all orchestration choices and positions Nomad as a simpler, more flexible alternative or complement to Kubernetes in some environments. It emphasizes that orchestrator choice should follow workload mix, staffing capacity, ecosystem needs, and operational context.

# Key Takeaways

- Kubernetes offers ecosystem breadth, while Nomad emphasizes operational simplicity and support for non-containerized workloads.
- Some organizations benefit from running multiple orchestrators instead of forcing all teams onto one platform.
- Orchestrator choice is ultimately about fit across technical and organizational constraints.
