# Summary

This source is an interview-driven article about where platform engineering is headed. It argues that many organizations focus too quickly on how to build an IDP without first clarifying why they need one, and it frames platform engineering as mostly cultural change with AI emerging as a new platform concern.

# Key Takeaways

- Platform engineering initiatives fail when they are treated as a tool purchase or a team label instead of a response to clear organizational need.
- The article stresses that platform engineering is heavily cultural, not just technical.
- AI and agentic tooling are presented as an emerging pressure on platform teams, especially for supporting new workloads and abstractions.
