# Summary

This source is a communication-focused essay about holding beliefs loosely enough to learn in difficult conversations. It emphasizes humility, truth-seeking, acknowledgment of uncertainty, and practical conversational moves that keep curiosity alive.

# Key Takeaways

- Productive disagreement starts with the assumption that one may be wrong about something.
- Curiosity is strengthened by valuing truth over identity-protective belief defense.
- The article recommends specific habits such as reset moments, acknowledging good points, and saying “I don’t know.”
