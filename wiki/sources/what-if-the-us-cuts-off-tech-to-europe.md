# Summary

This source is a speculative essay about Europe’s dependence on US cloud and AI infrastructure. It argues that Europe has underinvested in competitive AI infrastructure and models, then uses that claim to imagine the consequences if access to US cloud providers and related technology were politically curtailed.

# Key Takeaways

- The essay presents European dependence on US cloud platforms and AI infrastructure as a strategic vulnerability.
- It strongly contrasts US and Chinese AI momentum with what it portrays as Europe’s relative weakness.
- The source is argumentative and rhetorical rather than balanced policy analysis, but it surfaces a real dependency question.
